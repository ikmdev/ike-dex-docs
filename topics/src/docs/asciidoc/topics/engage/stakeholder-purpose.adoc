= Purpose

indexterm:[stakeholder engagement, Clinical Evidence Council]
indexterm:[knowledge exchange, DeX]
indexterm:[organizations, CDRH]
The purpose of the document serves as a record of stakeholder
engagement efforts taken and summarizes efforts surrounding
preparation of materials tied to FDA’s Clinical Evidence Council (CEC).
The team is supporting a presentation of Device Extension (DeX)
overview and use cases to the FDA Clinical Evidence Council (CEC).
The session is currently not scheduled after being moved from
December 4^th^,
2026. The primary audience encompassed individuals from across
the Center for Devices and Radiological Health (CDRH) including
Michelle Tarver,
head of CDRH.
This document will summarize the content the team built to
prepare for this session.
