= Background

Regulatory decisions and post-market safety monitoring
increasingly depend on real-world data (RWD),
but data that is accurate at its source often loses quality and
traceability as it moves downstream (Luu et al.,
2024). For laboratory devices,
key information such as identifiers,
interpretation guidance,
and performance limits are scattered across manuals,
databases,
and site-specific setups.
Because there is no shared source of truth for how device
outputs should be encoded,
results may not be comparable across sites,
drift may go undetected,
and performance monitoring at scale becomes difficult.
Device information lives across Global Unique Device
Identification Database (GUDID),
FDA premarket submission documentation (510(k)),
and package inserts,
and no single,
usable method currently brings these sources together into one view.

Integrated Knowledge Exchange (IKE) is an approach to data and
knowledge management that aims to maintain the meaning,
integrity,
and quality of data as it travels within and between healthcare systems.
IKE includes both a methodology and an accompanying ecosystem
of tools that manage disparate standards through a common,
centralized,
and repeatable representation of data.
Device Extensions (DeX) is one such tool,
designed to close this traceability gap for laboratory devices.
It is a knowledge repository of structured device metadata,
such as units of measure,
specimen type(s),
and reference range(s),
linked to laboratory terminologies including Logical
Observation Identifiers Names and Codes (LOINC®) and Systemized
Nomenclature of Medicine-Clinical Terms (SNOMED-CT®),
and to device identifiers in the GUDID.
DeX captures this information in a machine-readable format and
establishes a line of traceability from the device to its data.
That traceability supports comparability across sites and
provides a stronger,
clearer foundation for analytical and clinical decision support.
More broadly,
DeX offers a repeatable,
evidence-based framework for maintaining and scaling knowledge
about FDA-regulated products through automation and digital enablement.
