= Conclusion

indexterm:[automation, LLM assistance]
indexterm:[automation, rule-based extraction]
These early tests show that LLM-assisted processes can
meaningfully reduce the manual effort of building DeX records
across data collection and authoring,
along with device selection and data aggregation for scaling.
They also show where LLMs fall short:
they are not suited for verbatim reproduction of source documents,
which is why the team is shifting toward rule-based checks
where accuracy can be controlled.

indexterm:[workflow, SME validation]
indexterm:[knowledge exchange, DeX record]
Human review remains essential at every stage.
The team reviews LLM-extracted fields before they are accepted,
validates Komet Assistant drafts before they are published,
and confirms that each IVD device selected for scaling has the
source data needed for a complete record.
With these checkpoints in place,
LLMs can accelerate DeX authoring while the team remains
accountable for the accuracy and consistency of DeX records.
